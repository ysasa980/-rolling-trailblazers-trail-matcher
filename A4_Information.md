## GENERAL  FEEDBACK  FROM  A2  AND  A3  TO  CONSIDER

The following are tips to help all    groups improve their submissions in CSIT321:
- All groups must have strong internal branding, including a group name, logo, document
templates, etc. This should be shown throughout all your documentation – External
branding, if you have an industry client, could be different.
- Requirements need to show dependencies and the current order of development.
- For requirements, you should be considering more than just functional requirements.
- If you state that you are using agile, then you need to think about how you will be building
a minimum viable product (MVP) and then developing on it   in each iteration/sprint (this
should be what you are showing in A4, give some screenshot showing actual work not just
explain what is the sprint plan).
- Reflect on ‘Have you given yourself enough time to learn the new development stack?’

## A4  ITEMS  TO  CONSIDER

A4 should not be a long report. It needs to focus on your development is occurring, and your
initial working prototype or MVP. You can use the interfaces that you have presented in A3 with
any modifications identified during the presentation and from feedback with your sponsor/
supervisor or subject coordinator.
Groups need to identify their progress based on their system requirements. This can mean
identifying how far into the system development you are compared to where you wanted to be in
your initial planning. A table is a good way to show this, for example, ‘Requirements
Traceability Matrix’. ( Designed by yourself but may need ID, requirement capability,
requirement type (function or non-function), priority, status, notes...)
Groups need to provide their code (i.e. zipped file), a readme on how to get running and/or a
cloud-based version to review.
